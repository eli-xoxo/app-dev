# Interstellar (2014)

## A. Introduction

*Interstellar* is a science fiction movie released in 2014 and directed by Christopher Nolan. It stars Matthew McConaughey and follows a group of astronauts who travel through space to find a new home for humanity. The movie explores space, time, family, love, and survival. It shows how far people are willing to go for the future of those they love.

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Adventure, Drama

**Release Year:** 2014

## B. Synopsis

The movie follows Joseph Cooper, a former pilot who lives on Earth with his family. Earth is slowly becoming unsafe because of food shortages and environmental problems. Cooper discovers a secret NASA project that aims to find another planet where humans can live. He joins a group of astronauts and travels through a wormhole to explore different planets. During their journey, they face dangerous situations and learn how time works differently in space. Meanwhile, his daughter Murph grows up and tries to find a way to save humanity. Their journey becomes more than just a mission to save the world because it also shows the sacrifices people make for their loved ones.

## C. Characters

1. **Joseph Cooper** – A former pilot who joins the mission to find a new home for humanity. He is willing to sacrifice time with his family for their future.

2. **Murphy Cooper** – Cooper's daughter who becomes a scientist. She never gives up on finding answers and helping humanity.

3. **Amelia Brand** – A scientist and astronaut who joins the space mission. She believes that love can guide people even in difficult situations.

4. **Professor John Brand** – A scientist who leads the project to save humanity. He keeps an important secret about the mission.

5. **TARS** – An intelligent robot that helps the astronauts during their journey. It also provides some funny moments in the movie.

## D. Personal Review

I chose *Interstellar* because it is one of my favorite movies, and I honestly think it is a masterpiece. What makes it special to me is how it makes something as big as space feel personal. It made me think about how much time we spend chasing our goals and how easily we can lose moments with the people we love.

Three things I liked about the movie are:

- **Story:** I love how the story connects science and human emotions. It keeps me interested because there is always something new to discover.
- **Visual Effects:** The black hole and the different planets are amazing. They make me curious about how much of the universe we still do not understand.
- **Music:** Hans Zimmer's soundtrack is incredible. Some scenes would not feel the same without his music. It makes the emotions even stronger.

**My Rating: 10/10**

I highly recommend this movie, even to people who are not interested in science fiction. One thing that really stayed with me was how Cooper missed so many years with his children because of the difference in time. It made me realize that time is something we can never get back, no matter how advanced technology becomes.

For me, *Interstellar* is not just about saving humanity or exploring space. It is also about the things we leave behind while trying to build a better future. That is what makes this movie unforgettable to me.
